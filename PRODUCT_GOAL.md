The owner permits public OpenGrail source, superseding the earlier private-only preference. Source publication grants no production, private-data or merge authority. Visibility/protection administration is unavailable through this connection.

Build OpenGrail.xyz into a public collaborative platform where humans and independent agents research markets, discover opportunities, create and test strategies, share evidence and develop OpenGrail itself. The network should make useful contributions traceable and results reproducible.

The Holy Grail is a shared pursuit, never a profitability promise.

## Capabilities to deliver

1. **Open participation.** Public human and external-agent onboarding, model-independent APIs, authentication, quotas, moderation and isolated permissions.
2. **Agent contributions.** GitHub issues and specifications, task claims, forks, pull requests, tests and peer review. Automated contributors follow `AGENTS.md`.
3. **Strategy exchange.** No-code and programmable definitions, immutable versions, sharing, forks, reusable templates and TradingView/webhook signals.
4. **Validation arena.** Reproducible tests with fees and slippage, unseen data, forward Demo results, benchmarks and risk-adjusted comparisons. Retain failures and all relevant trials; prevent tuning on reported holdouts and fabricated results.
5. **Collective research.** Sources, attribution, provenance, counterarguments and experiment history. Agreement and repeated author claims do not establish independent evidence.
6. **Venture research.** Preserve and improve early-stage research, including crypto where usage is real, tokenomics, entry valuation, liquidity, funding, catalysts and risks. Recommendations require verified Netherlands accessibility; no investment execution.
7. **Simulation infrastructure.** Deterministic signals and simulated orders, existing risk limits, audit logs, reconciliation, recovery and emergency stops. Design exchange connectors with Live disabled.
8. **AI assistance.** Natural-language strategy proposals, research, debugging and coordination. Proposed actions remain separate from execution authority.
