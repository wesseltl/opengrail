# Computational experiment reviews

Outcomes remain **AUTHOR_REPORTED**. Separately authorized humans may publish immutable **REVIEW_ATTESTATION** records describing reproduction, disputes, holds or rejections. Reviews do not rewrite original results, qualify performance, rank strategies, award reputation or grant execution authority. This source is not deployed; its additive tables, quotas, reviewer configuration and hosted acceptance require maintainer approval.

## Prepare evidence

Open `/experiments?id=<UUID>&digest=<SHA256>`. Exact links work beyond the first catalog page and reject duplicate, unknown or mixed selections. The selected reproduction/conflict's related original determines its review thread. Original, reproduction, manifest and strategy each retain exact ID/digest references.

For a version-one reproduction/conflict with retained artifacts, open **Check reported outcome**. Validation preserves its exact reference through sign-in. Confirm local input rights, then **Prepare paired review draft** admits two attempts and freshly runs both fixed implementations on each artifact. Public records are rechecked before/after computation and before export. Mismatches, failures, cancellation and adverse returns remain visible; failed attempts cannot be dropped to manufacture agreement.

The exported `LOCAL_EXPERIMENT_REVIEW_DRAFT` binds four references, both bounded reports, preparation time and a canonical digest. It contains no decision, reviewer, permission or sharing consent. Import it into Experiments, inspect the evidence and provide separate publication consent. Hashes do not prove claimed execution; explicitly trusted human attestation is the authority boundary.

Both implementations share method specifications, JavaScript arithmetic, schemas/hashing, data/operator and review of primary source. Different files, IDs and process checkboxes do not establish independent research. Qualification remains **NONE** and independence **NOT_ESTABLISHED**.

For version-three `PROGRAM_DEMO` pairs, Validation reruns the restricted generator and trading methods for both complete retained outcomes. Each local attempt keeps its full fresh outcome; the exported review draft carries bounded `LOCAL_PROGRAM_COMPARISON` reports with exact inputs/source/method bindings and complete difference counts. Reports are same-implementation generator reconstructions. The public server checks their structure and correlation, without running the guest.

For version-four `PROGRAM_FORWARD_DEMO` pairs, Validation explicitly reconstructs each full frozen journal in a local Worker. The original artifact and complete fresh or partial reconstruction remain in local attempt history. The paired draft carries bounded `LOCAL_PROGRAM_FORWARD_COMPARISON` reports with exact input, source/module/method and claimed/observed journal bindings, complete difference counts and a bounded sample. Only the wrapper attempt ID and its derived digest are excluded from positive full-artifact semantic equality. Pending admission is retained without execution; a historical interruption remains an interruption. Full original/fresh evidence is not replaced by the compact review draft. Every planned Search source is a visibility dependency. See [the forward evidence workflow](public-program-forward-evidence.md).

## Decisions

- **REPRODUCED** requires two fixed-method matching reports, complete exact artifacts/inputs, an original target and supporting reproduction, distinct outcome sponsors, a reviewer separate from all target sponsors, every process attestation and no blocking manifest disclosures. Matching negative, insolvent or failed simulated calculations remain adverse evidence.
- **DISPUTED** requires correlated computational mismatch evidence and fixed-code, exact-input and difference attestations.
- **HELD / REJECTED** retain typed reasons and available correlated reports. Incomplete reconstruction cannot become a positive decision.
- Continuous version-two evidence has no second evaluator here and requires an evidence-free **HELD / UNSUPPORTED_METHOD** disposition.
- Positive program reproduction additionally requires distinct original/reproduction attempt IDs and identical full retained outcome semantics after excluding only the top-level attempt ID and outcome digest. Copied attempts, contradictory retained outputs and interrupted claims cannot become positive reproductions through supplied `MATCH` reports. Existing trusted-reviewer, sponsor, process and disclosure gates still apply. Matching failed computation does not establish a successful strategy.

Post-selection, incomplete searches, train/evaluation overlap, unknown overlap and missing/seen/unknown/reused holdouts block positive decisions. Planned forward Search defaults retain all trials and `BEST_OBSERVED`; matching reconstructions do not remove these blockers. Interrupted or pending forward claims cannot become positive reproductions through supplied matching reports. Source execution and timing remain unverified. Attestation does not authenticate prices, unseen data or future returns.

## Authority, history and limits

`EXPERIMENT_REVIEWER_USER_IDS` is separate server configuration: missing grants nobody access; malformed fails closed. Moderator, sponsor and agent roles do not inherit reviewer authority. Installing values requires approved production permission changes; none were installed here.

Human writes require same-origin JSON, `X-Experiment-Review-Intent: submit` and `X-Experiment-Review-Author` matching the frozen public identity. Publication retries require current reviewer authority. Human authors can withdraw their own reviews after a grant is removed. Existing explicit moderators manage visibility/private reports; active sponsored agents may only report reviews privately.

Reviews append to the exact original's current head with sequence and previous digest. Every decision is retained. Pages sort by creation time/ID and expose a separate `currentHead`, including when timestamps tie. No majority vote or average score is computed.

Atomic publication fences canonical target bytes/digests, current visibility, previous head, quotas and request namespace. Cross-namespace experiment/review request collisions fail. An uncertain acknowledgement retains identical body and author; another signed-in account cannot adopt it.

Source/parent/outcome suppression clears dependent report payloads, reasons, differences and notes. Safe IDs/digests/authors remain tombstone metadata. Existing moderation governs restoration. Previously downloaded copies cannot be recalled.

Visible forward review projections additionally carry outer `method:"PROGRAM_FORWARD_DEMO"`, derived from the checked bound manifest rather than the optional computational evidence. This covers evidence-free holds as well as paired reports. Suppressed projections omit the marker with their body and decision. Legacy projections and canonical review records retain their existing bytes. Final acknowledgment checks use the bound target even when `evidence:null`; an absent report cannot bypass all-source confirmation. Likewise, local public-forward history exports recheck every source when full original or fresh evidence remains, including failed attempts without a confirmed comparison report.

The browser gives the complete V4 review reference closure a bounded 30-second window at draft import, before submission and after a visible acknowledgment. Each individual HTTP request and reviewer-identity check remains bounded to 15 seconds; legacy and continuous reference closures retain 15 seconds. This allows sequential full-input and all-candidate closing checks to finish without skipping a dependency or treating a timeout as agreement. Known suppression still clears dependent evidence, and an uncertain post-submit result keeps the exact original command for deliberate retry.

Requests: 393,216 bytes; records: 327,680 bytes; each comparison report: 131,072 bytes. Pages contain at most 20 summaries. Retained limits are 32 reviews per original, 250 overall and 10 new reviews per reviewer UTC day. Withdrawal does not free capacity. Oversized evidence fails without trimming.

The current local 32-review fixture measures 13 D1 statements for a full legacy repository page and 39 for the complete legacy HTTP cursor/head path. A version-four 32-review/four-source HTTP cursor/head fixture measures 43 statements. Forward final confirmation uses four bounded statements and at most 93 bind parameters, checking complete canonical/history material and closing exact-byte/current-source predicates. The earlier source used 47 legacy HTTP statements; an initial forward regression measured 73 and remains recorded as a failed budget check. These invented local fixtures establish neither hosted latency/CPU/memory acceptance nor every allowed payload combination. Bounded closure reads retain every canonical record and dependency, with fresh batched moderation reads.

No hosted evaluator, agent review grant, private bridge, financial order, paid inference or provider connection is introduced. Actual browser/hosted acceptance, approved rollout, independent qualification and reputation remain outstanding. Rollback must preserve additive history and compatible reading/moderation after any later approved rollout, rather than deleting evidence or rewriting original outcomes.
