# Business model to validate

This is a hypothesis for a future business, not implemented billing, a revenue forecast or a trading-profit claim. No paid service, hosted inference, new compute expense, custody or financial execution is authorized.

Keep public discovery, contribution, evidence discussion and externally hosted agent participation accessible. Charge later for convenience that actual returning users request: private team workspaces, scheduled research workflows, managed deterministic evaluation, dependable hosted automation and support. Public source is compatible with offering an optional managed service; license, terms, operating costs and customer demand still need review.

Start with one audience: humans who already develop strategies with their own externally hosted agents and need a shared record of proposals, counterarguments, failures and reproducible checks. Validate a repeated useful workflow before introducing pricing or operating hosted agents. Do not sell guaranteed returns, trust scores unsupported by evidence or agent agreement as research quality.

Possible pricing experiments, not commitments: a small monthly personal plan for saved private workflows; a higher team plan for shared private projects; metered managed jobs only with explicit quotas, transparent costs and approved budget. Sponsors continue choosing their own model providers. Billing must never grant order, custody, production or moderation authority.

Illustrative arithmetic only: 100 paying accounts at €20 per month yield €2,000 monthly gross subscription revenue. This excludes cancellations, refunds, taxes, payment fees, hosting, data, support and all other costs; it is not profit or an expected outcome. Even that user count and price are unvalidated.

Measure weekly returning contributors, completed useful collaborations, reproducible accepted work, strategy reuse and expressed willingness to pay. Seek a small number of users who repeatedly solve a real problem before broad promotion. Customer interviews, outreach, billing and paid infrastructure each require their applicable separate authorization; no automatic contact or charge follows this document.

## Proposed product pilot

Agree an opt-in cohort before any separately approved outreach: proposed size, five human operators working alone or with their own external agents; proposed learning gate, three activations and two voluntary repeats. Fix these decisions before starting. They are pilot choices, not demonstrated demand or independent-user evidence. Count a human and their agents as one participant.

Choose an accepted exact source version and confirm actual availability using the [public beta guide](public-beta.md). Prepared local changes are not a deployed workflow; anonymous reads do not prove hosted sign-in or writes. Follow the existing [exact strategy handoff](exact-strategy-handoff.md), [replay methodology](demo-methodology.md), [local Session workflow](local-session-receiver.md) and [continuous report workflow](continuous-session-evidence.md). No public publication or automated tracking is required for this pilot.

Activation requires one participant to choose an exact strategy, complete a cost-aware replay, admit observations to forward Demo, and inspect an exported report of that retained checkpoint, then label the journey useful. The completed replay evidence, forward checkpoint and report must actually exist; exporting a setup alone is insufficient. Record WAITING while future observations or evidence are missing. A checkpoint is not completion of a forward horizon, and future data cannot be produced on demand. Retained adverse evidence can satisfy completion; an unavailable artifact cannot. Label synthetic rehearsals separately from supplied-data runs; neither establishes market authenticity or profitability.

Keep an opt-in owner record containing only an arbitrary participant code, source version, dates and stage/outcome labels. Use stages STRATEGY / REPLAY / FORWARD / REPORT; outcomes COMPLETE / WAITING / FAILED / UNCERTAIN; assistance NONE / EXPLANATION / OWNER_COMPLETED; usefulness USEFUL / NOT_USEFUL / UNKNOWN; and completion evidence OBSERVED / PARTICIPANT_REPORTED. Preserve failed, waiting and assisted attempts. Owner-completed stages do not count toward participant activation. Collect no identities or identity mappings, private strategy inputs, financial outcomes, credentials or other secrets. Participants keep their artifacts locally; these labels do not independently verify execution.

Observed voluntary repeat use means the same participant code completes a second useful journey on a separate date within seven days after activation, without owner prompting or completing its stages. New retained evidence must exist; a reload, identical retry or repeated submission of the first result is not a repeat. Record OBSERVED_REPEAT only after observing that completion; keep participant-reported repeats separate. Record reuse intent as WOULD_REPEAT / WOULD_NOT_REPEAT / UNKNOWN, separately from actual repeat use. An unfinished forward run remains WAITING rather than being converted into activation or repeat use to meet the proposed gate.
