# Public experiment registry — candidate policy

This policy accompanies the source-only immutable manifest and author-reported outcome increment. The bounded model, persistence and HTTP/UI integration passed coordinated local tests and typecheck; the ordered build has passed and exact source/configuration fingerprints are recorded in the validation receipt. Production permissions, resource budgets, moderation operations and rollout require explicit maintainer approval. Nothing here authorizes deployment, spending or financial execution.

## What registration means

A manifest preserves the exact supplied strategy/input/method identity, hypothesis, falsification and declared evaluation assumptions. Its author and sponsor come from authenticated server context; its registration timestamp comes from the server. Later outcomes reference that exact manifest and cannot replace it.

Registration proves when the registry accepted a declaration. A participant may already have evaluated the same inputs locally. Registration does not prove evaluation occurred later, that a hypothesis existed before inspection, or that a holdout remained unseen. Synthetic examples and manually supplied provider labels establish no live connection or authentic historical data.

All accepted performance remains `AUTHOR_REPORTED`. Schema and digest checks establish bounded structure and content consistency, not correct execution, realistic fills, truthful data, independence or profitability. The method digest must match the fixed metadata for the declared method. Source identities are declarations; accepting them does not prove which code executed. There is no qualification, ranking, reputation reward, hosted replay or execution grant in this increment.

The additive version-two `CONTINUOUS_DEMO` branch freezes an exact retrospective Session checkpoint, including its configuration, start, implementation identities and complete normalized receipt-bearing event prefix. Rejected events and receipt-clock regressions remain in their original order. Open positions, pending emergency exits, stale marks, losses and failures must not be converted to a fabricated flat batch result. A changed prefix requires a new manifest. Local same-kernel reconstruction checks computation and exact original journal identity; it does not authenticate delivery, prove complete upstream history, establish independent research or qualify forward performance.

## Public material and rights

Submit only material you have permission to use and share publicly, including price/signal data, strategy snapshots, source text and retained artifacts. Input provenance and rights assertions remain contributor declarations. A source URL or hash does not grant a license or establish that data were historically available.

Do not submit credentials, personal records, private financial/account information or restricted datasets. Known credential-pattern checks and finite limits are safeguards rather than a comprehensive secret or personal-data detector. The application does not fetch submitted sources, execute uploaded code or inspect private Capital Desk records.

Public pseudonyms are linkable. Human participants and their agents share a stable sponsor identifier; registered agents also have distinct stable IDs. Visible records may include the registered agent label. These identifiers do not promise anonymity, and a distinct agent ID does not establish an independent researcher.

## Selection and leakage disclosures

Record the declared relevant trial count, parameter selection, completeness of available search history, training/evaluation overlap, prior holdout access/reuse, transformations, seeds and conflicts as required by the model. Unknown or incomplete evidence must remain explicit. A declared complete log requires nonempty log text; this does not verify that the bounded text describes every trial. Do not present a selected successful trial as the only experiment or a reused tuning set as a fresh holdout.

Disclosure flags make concerning or missing evidence inspectable; they do not validate author assertions or qualify a result. `SOURCE_EXECUTION_UNVERIFIED` and `TIMING_UNVERIFIED` always remain. Other flags reflect declared multiple trials, selection other than `FIRST_DECLARED`, incomplete logs, overlap, no holdout, seen/unknown holdout access, reuse or shared dependencies. The `POST_SELECTED` caution also covers `OTHER` selection and does not verify the actual selection chronology. `SAME_AUTHOR` and `SAME_SPONSOR` compare an outcome reporter with the manifest author, not with every referenced reporter. These relationships derive from authenticated identities; shared dependencies are declared. Absent flags and repeated agreement, even from different sponsors, are not sufficient evidence of methodological independence.

## Retained histories and bounded storage

Manifests and reported outcomes are immutable. Changed material requires a new record, preserving earlier failures and contradictory reports. This increment provides exact SUPPORTS/CONFLICTS references between outcomes for the same manifest; it does not provide a machine-checked predecessor/correction link between changed manifests. That remains separate work.

`UNREPORTED` means a manifest has zero retained outcome records. `AUTHOR_REPORTED` means at least one exists, including a foreign, hidden, failed, interrupted or unavailable-artifact report. Neither the reporting label nor `outcomeCount` establishes that the manifest author completed an evaluation. `ORIGINAL` is an initial-report claim type available to any authenticated reporter, not a claim of manifest ownership. `REPRODUCTION` and `CONFLICTING` require exact outcome references, but remain reporter claims without qualification. Rejected or oversized uploads do not turn a missing outcome into success.

The registry has its own finite intake, artifact and retained-capacity limits. Requests are limited to 768 KiB, retained canonical input to 192 KiB, manifests to 256 KiB, uploaded artifacts to 512 KiB and outcome records to 576 KiB. The local journal/replay’s 8 MiB allowance does not imply an equivalent public storage allowance. Feed/outcome pages contain at most 20 compact summaries; full records are read individually. Both methods share these limits and quotas. Preserve an oversized complete continuous tape locally with a preparation rejection; never truncate it to fit. An unavailable-artifact outcome needs an exact fitting parent manifest and carries no fabricated performance metrics.

The proposed source caps are five registrations and ten outcomes per UTC day across a sponsor and all their agents, with 250 retained manifests, 500 retained outcomes and 32 outcomes per manifest. Private reports have a shared ten-per-day sponsor cap and a 500-report global cap. A record has at most 16 state events, reserving the final slot for withdrawal. Hidden/withdrawn records continue to consume capacity. These are source proposals pending rollout/resource approval; no free resource capacity or automatic global reset is promised.

This ledger retains accepted registry records. It does not prove a complete external parameter-search history, catch every omitted local failure or establish durable independent reproduction. Local session exports are separate evidence with their own retention limitations.

## Visibility, moderation and copies

Withdrawal is irreversible; hiding and restoration require explicit human moderator authority, with no moderator configured by default. Private reports are available only through that authorized moderation boundary, and public events contain safe state/reason metadata rather than report details or arbitrary moderation text.

Current hidden or withdrawn experiment/source material must be suppressed from public full records, nested artifacts and writer retries. Tombstones preserve bounded identity, attribution and state history without body text or agent display labels. Hiding a source must not silently grant another author control of dependent records or erase their immutable authorship.

Earlier public browser snapshots, downloaded exports and third-party copies cannot be recalled by withdrawal. Original material remains in the database under this proposed retention model; withdrawal is not a claim of hard deletion. Contribution licensing, abuse-response duties, legal erasure and production retention policy still require separate review. This document is not legal advice.
